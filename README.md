# 🌐 Sedea AI Community Hub

Welcome to the **Sedea AI Community** repository! This is the central space for the Sedea community to connect directly with the Sedea team, share ideas, and help shape the future of our ecosystem. 

Whether you are a long-time user, a curious developer, or just getting started, we are thrilled to have you here.

---

## 🚀 How to Interact with Us

We want to hear from you! Here is how you can use this repository to communicate with the Sedea team:

### ❓ Ask Questions & Start Discussions
Have a question about how Sedea works, best practices, or upcoming plans? 
* Head over to our **[Discussions Tab](https://github.com/sedea-ai/community/discussions)**.
* Check if your question has already been answered, or start a new thread under the relevant category (e.g., Q&A, Ideas, General).

### 💡 Request a Feature
Have a brilliant idea to make Sedea better? We track all feature requests right here.
1. Go to the **[Issues Tab](https://github.com/sedea-ai/community/issues)**.
2. Click **New Issue** and select the **Feature Request** template.
3. Provide as much context as possible about *what* you want and *why* it would be useful.

### 🐛 Report a Bug / Submit Issues
Notice something acting strangely? Help us fix it!
1. Check the existing issues to see if someone else has already reported it.
2. If not, open a **New Issue** using the **Bug Report** template.
3. Please include steps to reproduce the issue, what you expected to happen, and any relevant logs or screenshots.

## 🤝 Community Guidelines (Code of Conduct)

To ensure this remains a welcoming, safe, and productive space for everyone, we ask that all community members follow a few simple rules:

* **Be Respectful:** Treat fellow community members and the Sedea team with kindness and empathy.
* **Stay on Topic:** Keep discussions focused on Sedea, its tools, and its ecosystem.
* **No Spam:** Avoid self-promotion, advertising, or repetitive posting.
* **Look First:** Search existing discussions and issues before posting a new one to keep the repository clean.

> ⚠️ **Note:** Any behavior violating our full [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) will result in comments being moderated or access being restricted.
